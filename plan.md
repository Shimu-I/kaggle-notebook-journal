## 🗺️ The Ultimate Beginner Kaggle Competition Path

### 1. The Classic Starter: Titanic (Tabular Classification)

* **What it is:** You get a spreadsheet of passengers (age, gender, ticket class) and predict who survives the shipwreck. This teaches you binary classification ($0$ or $1$).
* **Best YouTube Videos:**
* [Kaggle Titanic Competition Walkthrough by Greg Hogg](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&source=gmail&q=https://youtu.be/8yZMXCaFshs) – A brilliant, clear coding walkthrough showing you exactly how to train a model and export your `submission.csv` file.
* [Titanic Data Science Walkthrough by Ken Jee](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&source=gmail&q=https://youtu.be/I3FBJdiExcg) – Ken Jee breaks down his thought process, feature engineering, and how he iterates to get a better score.



### 2. The Next Step: Spaceship Titanic (Tabular Classification with Missing Data)

* **What it is:** A modern spin on the classic Titanic. A cosmic anomaly teleports passengers to another dimension. It uses the exact same math as the original Titanic but features much messier, modern data with lots of missing values. It's the perfect training ground before moving on.
* **Best YouTube Video:**
* [Spaceship Titanic Complete Walkthrough by Data Science Jay](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&source=gmail&q=https://youtu.be/u887mKbeIis) – Focuses heavily on data cleaning and handling missing values, which is exactly what you need to master next.



### 3. The Text Starter: Natural Language Processing with Disaster Tweets (NLP)

* **What it is:** You are given a dataset of real tweets. You have to build a machine learning model that predicts whether a tweet is talking about a *real* disaster (like a wildfire or earthquake) or just using a figure of speech (like "this concert is fire!").
* **Best YouTube Video:**
* [NLP with Disaster Tweets Tutorial by Mr. DB](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&source=gmail&q=https://youtu.be/W8y7EisvPzc) – Walks you through turning words into numbers (tokenization) and training a basic text classifier.



### 4. The Image Starter: Digit Recognizer (Computer Vision)

* **What it is:** The famous MNIST dataset. You are given thousands of 28x28 pixel images of handwritten numbers (0-9). You will build a Neural Network to look at the pixels and guess what number was written.
* **Best YouTube Video:**
* [Kaggle Digit Recognizer with TensorFlow/Keras by Nicholas Renotte](https://www.google.com/url?sa=E&source=gmail&q=https://youtu.be/j-3vuBynnOE) – Nicholas is fantastic at teaching Deep Learning. He will guide you through building a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) from scratch.
